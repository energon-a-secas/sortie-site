# Sortie — product one-pager

**What it is:** Boot a mech console, forge parts, fire burst modes

**Who it is for:** Someone who likes mecha cockpit interfaces enough to want to
sit inside one and fiddle with it. Not a designer at work; a person with a spare
twenty minutes who wants to draw a robot arm on a 12 by 12 grid and watch a
console take it seriously.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** Evening, one browser tab open on a wide monitor, probably a second
screen. The room is dim. The visitor pokes at the console, unlocks it, watches
the parts come online, then spends longer than expected in the forge. Dark,
dense and loud on purpose: this is a cockpit, not a dashboard.

**What done looks like:** The visitor has a frame that is theirs. At least one
forged part on it, an accent they picked, and either a share link or a poster
they wanted to keep.
